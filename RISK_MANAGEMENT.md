# Risk policy

**Paper only. Default stopped.** An operator enables individual bots and types `RESUME PAPER TRADING` to authorize new entries. API resume does not change limits. Pausing a bot prevents its new submissions; use global emergency stop to cancel eligible existing entries. Circuit breaker and order actions are persisted. A trade can still fill while a stop or cancellation is in flight.

- Risk-based sizing uses decimal arithmetic: 0.5% of account equity divided by price-to-stop distance, then caps at 10% equity, available cash, buying power and 10% of displayed ask size (IEX sizes used conservatively). Round down to the permitted lot. Stop is roughly 2 ATR from the limit; the target is at least twice the initial risk before fees. Verify the broker asset, permissions, spread and data freshness.
- A dedicated account may have only one application position at a time. Any broker inventory or open order, including external ones, blocks additional entries, as does any unresolved intent. No pyramiding, leverage, shorting, options, or live mode. This also limits crypto at entry to the tighter 10% position cap; actual mark-to-market exposure can drift.
- Risk trip levels are losses of 2% daily and 4% weekly, measured from the first successful persisted equity snapshot on or after UTC midnight or Monday UTC. The baseline includes realized and unrealized changes to account equity. It is not cash-flow adjusted. Missing observations at period start mean this is a prospective baseline, not a reconstruction of opening equity. The dashboard's broker daily change uses Alpaca's previous equity and may differ from the breaker.
- An emergency or auto stop blocks new buys and attempts cancellation of managed outstanding entries, subject to broker reachability and the next worker cycle. It is **not** a liquidation command. The worker still reconciles and attempts exits of attributable positions with available quantity. Exits are software-monitored, not broker-native brackets, so there is no guaranteed stop price or protection when the worker or broker is down. Stops and take profits use fresh bids, with partial-fill reconciliation and spread/price validation. No blind exit is sent on stale or missing data.
- A claimed submission with unknown outcome is not retried automatically. An unknown outcome can freeze new orders indefinitely after a broker outage. Read the [incident guide](INCIDENT_RESPONSE.md).

These limits are conservative application defaults, not guaranteed-safe settings or recommendations. Correlation-aware portfolio controls, drawdown circuit breakers, loss-streak cooldown and trailing/partial exit policies are not implemented in this baseline.
